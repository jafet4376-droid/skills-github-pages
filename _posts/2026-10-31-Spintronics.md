Spintronics: the future of electronics beyond charge




















10








Spintronics (spin electronics) is a field of physics and engineering that exploits not only the electrical charge of electrons, but also their intrinsic angular momentum, known as spin, to store, process, and transmit information.

It is particularly interesting because conventional electronics primarily manipulate electron charge, whereas spintronics introduces another degree of freedom that can be exploited for computing and memory.
1. The fundamental idea
An electron has two fundamental properties relevant here:
- Electric charge: allows it to participate in electrical currents.
- Spin: an intrinsic quantum-mechanical property associated with angular momentum and a magnetic moment.
